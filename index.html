The case for paper

Five reasons to bag it in paper

♻️

Recycles in your community

Most curbside programs accept paper bags. Plastic film often isn’t collected and ends up in landfills or incinerators.

🌊

Less harm to wildlife

Marine animals mistake plastic bags for food. Paper degrades before it can choke turtles, birds, and fish.

🌱

Made from renewable fiber

Paper comes from trees grown on managed forests—and increasingly from recycled content. Plastic comes from fossil fuels.

🔥

No toxic microplastics

Plastic bags break into microplastics that enter soil, water, and our food chain. Paper doesn’t leave that legacy.

🏙️

Cleaner streets & parks

Wind-blown plastic bags litter neighborhoods for years. Paper tears down and is picked up or composted much faster.

Real-world impact

Every paper bag is a vote for cleaner places

Cities worldwide are restricting single-use plastic bags. Shoppers who switch to paper—or better, reusable totes—help reduce the estimated 500 billion plastic bags used globally each year.

  • Ask your store to offer paper at checkout
  • Reuse paper bags for lunch, gifts, or storage
  • Recycle only when the bag is clean and dry
  • Support local bans on single-use plastic
